FAQ

Frequently asked questions about E-menu.

What is an E-menu?

E-menu is a digital menu customers open on their phone by scanning a QR code. It is designed for cafés, restaurants, hotels, and food businesses.

Can I update menu items and prices instantly?

Yes. The product is designed so businesses can update menu information and availability, helping customers see the menu that is currently active.

Will customers need to install an app?

No. Customers can scan a QR code and open the live menu in their normal phone browser.

What does the Free plan include?

The Free plan provides a limited starter template selection, business details, menu item management, a published menu link, and QR code access.

Can I use my existing menu design?

Standard is planned to support custom menu design uploads and conversion of existing menu artwork into an E-menu experience.

How will AI customisation work?

Premium AI design assistance is planned for a future product phase. It will let businesses describe preferred changes through a guided chat or voice-based experience.

Can I share the menu on WhatsApp and social media?

Yes. Each published menu is intended to have a shareable link, alongside QR, image, and PDF-ready delivery options.

Is a real login available on this website now?

This launch-site prototype demonstrates the sign-up and login experience using browser storage only. Production login, OTP email, payment, QR generation, and hosted menu delivery require secure backend APIs before public release.
